A page can shut someone out because of vision, hearing, movement, speech, or how they think and remember. It can also shut out someone with a broken arm, lost glasses, bright sun, or a loud room.

The person is not a mascot and not an enemy. They are trying to complete the [same task](/learn/comparable-access/).

W3C’s *How People with Disabilities Use the Web* is the official set of stories. Cognitive and learning disabilities are only partly covered by WCAG success criteria; see the library’s [cognitive](/docs/cognitive/) notes after the profile passes.
