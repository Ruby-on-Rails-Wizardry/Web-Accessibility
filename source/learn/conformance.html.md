---
title: Conformance is a page and a process
description: A passing widget is not a passing site. One failing step can fail the whole sequence.
node: conformance
---

WCAG conformance is claimed for **full pages** and, for a process (buy, apply, log in), for **every step**. A beautiful, labeled login button does not make the site conform.

GSA’s 508 reading: fail one of the applicable success criteria and the **page** fails. A process fails if any step fails.

Official explanation: [Understanding WCAG 2 conformance](/docs/wcag/conformance/).
