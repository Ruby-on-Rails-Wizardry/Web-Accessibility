---
title: Comparable access to what we offer
description: If we publish a task or a service, people should be able to complete that same task — not a bolted-on side door.
node: comparable-access
---

Most of this tree is about **what we offer**: a form, a video on a site, a federal page. Respect here means **comparable access** to that offering.

It does not mean everyone can hold every job. That is [worth is not identical capacity](/learn/not-identical-capacity/).

A “special” alternate is not automatically respect. WCAG conforming alternate versions and 508 alternatives are **tightly constrained**. Prefer one interface that works.

## Official texts

- [Section 508, 29 U.S.C. § 794d](https://www.section508.gov/manage/laws-and-policies/section-508-law/) — access and use **comparable** to that available to others
- [CRPD Article 9](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/convention-rights-persons-disabilities) — access to ICT on an equal basis with others
- [WAI introduction](https://www.w3.org/WAI/fundamentals/accessibility-intro/) — equal access and equal opportunity
- Local: [Revised Section 508](/docs/section-508/)
