After [errors are identified](/learn/form-errors/): some mistakes cannot be a shrug. **3.3.4** applies when the user is committing to a legal or financial transaction, or modifying/deleting user-controllable data.

At least one of: reversible, checked for input errors with a chance to correct, or a confirmation step that reviews the data.

Official pair: Sufficient [G98](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G98), [G99](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G99), [G155](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G155). Understanding: [Error Prevention (Legal, Financial, Data)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/error-prevention-legal-financial-data).

## Contrast

**Worse:** “Delete my account” is a single click with no confirm and no undo.

**Better:** A review page (“You are about to delete account 123. Type DELETE to confirm”) or a time-limited undo.
