---
title: More than one way to find a page
description: A set of pages needs more than a single nav path. Search, a sitemap, or a related-links list is the second way.
node: multiple-ways
rule: Provide at least two ways to find a page within a set of pages.
---

If the only way to reach a page is one deep menu, people who cannot use that menu are stuck. **2.4.5** asks for **two** ways within a set of pages (nav + search, nav + sitemap, …).

A step in a **process** (checkout) is excepted — you are not required to offer a second way into “enter CVV.” [Skip past repeated blocks](/learn/skip-link/) is a different criterion (bypass on one page). Section 508 **excepts non-web documents** from 2.4.5 (E205.4). Web sites are in.

<div class="example example--bad" markdown="1">

## Bad

Department pages exist only as items in a hover mega-menu. There is no search and no A–Z list.

The menu is the only path. People who cannot use that menu cannot reach the pages.

</div>

<div class="example example--good" markdown="1">

## Good

The same menu, plus site search that returns those pages (or a sitemap that lists them).

Two ways: browse and search. Either one finds the department page.

</div>

</div>

## Not a pass

A search box that never returns the department pages is not a second way. A sitemap that 404s is not a second way. Hover versus click on the **same** menu is still one way.

## Official

**2.4.5 Multiple Ways** (AA). In `section-508-web` (WCAG 2.0 AA; web only — non-web documents excepted under E205.4), `ada-title-ii` (2.1 AA), and `wcag-22-aa` (2.2 AA).

Sufficient [G125](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G125), [G161](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G161), [G185](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G185). Understanding: [Multiple Ways](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/multiple-ways).
