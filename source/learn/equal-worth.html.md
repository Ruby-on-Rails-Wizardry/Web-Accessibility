**All men are created equal** — here, **all people have value and should be treated with respect.**

A concrete pattern later on this tree (a `for`/`id` pair, a meaningful `alt`) is how that respect shows up in an interface. Section 508, ADA Title II, and WCAG are **bars**. They are not the why.

This is not a claim that every person can perform every job. That distinction is the next node: [Worth is not identical capacity](/learn/not-identical-capacity/).

## Official texts

- [Declaration of Independence](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-transcript) — “all men are created equal… unalienable Rights”
- [UDHR Article 1](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights) — “All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights.”
- [CRPD](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/convention-rights-persons-disabilities) — inherent dignity; accessibility as part of equal enjoyment of rights
- [ADA findings, 42 U.S.C. § 12101](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12101) — disability does not diminish the right to fully participate

American restatements of the same creed (not accessibility statutes): Lincoln at Gettysburg; King in 1963, quoting the Declaration.

## Voice on this tree

Say **accessibility**, or the specific name (WCAG, Section 508, ADA, this pattern). Do not frame the work as choosing a side. A miss names something to study, not a kind of person.
