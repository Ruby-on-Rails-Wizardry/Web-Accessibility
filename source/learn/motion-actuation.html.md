---
title: Motion is not the only input
description: Shake-to-undo and tilt-to-steer need a button (or other control), and a way to turn motion off.
node: motion-actuation
---

WCAG **2.1**. A phone on a wheelchair mount cannot be shaken. Tremor must not fire the action.

Functionality operated by **device or user motion** also needs a user-interface control, and responding to motion can be disabled — except an accessibility-supported interface or an essential motion (the pedometer *is* the step).

Hooks: **2.5.4 Motion Actuation**. Official: [Understanding Motion Actuation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/motion-actuation). Sufficient [G213](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G213).

## Contrast

**Worse:** “Shake to undo” with no other undo.

**Better:** An Undo button, and a setting that ignores shake.
