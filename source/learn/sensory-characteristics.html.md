[Color is not the only cue](/learn/color-not-only-cue/) is the color case. This node is the rest of **1.3.3**: instructions cannot rely **only** on sensory characteristics (shape, size, visual location, orientation, or sound).

You may still say “the round green button labeled Save” — the name is the thing that stands alone.

Official pair: Failure [F14](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F14); Sufficient [G96](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G96). Understanding: [Sensory Characteristics](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/sensory-characteristics).

## Contrast

**Worse:** “Press the button on the right when you hear the chime.”

**Better:** “Press **Save** (the right-hand button) when the status says Ready.”
