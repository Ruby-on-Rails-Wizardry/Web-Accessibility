---
title: Not only shape, size, or sound
description: “Click the round button” or “when you hear the beep” is not enough if that is the only instruction.
node: sensory-characteristics
rule: Do not give instructions that rely only on shape, size, location, orientation, or sound.
---

[Color is not the only cue](/learn/color-not-only-cue/) is the color case. This node is the rest of **1.3.3**: instructions cannot rely **only** on sensory characteristics (shape, size, visual location, orientation, or sound). People who do not see the layout, or who do not hear the chime, never get the instruction.

You may still say “the round green button labeled Save” — the name is the thing that stands alone.

<div class="examples" markdown="1">

<div class="example example--bad" markdown="1">

## Bad

“Press the button on the right when you hear the chime.”

The only cues are location and sound. Neither is available to everyone.

</div>

<div class="example example--good" markdown="1">

## Good

“Press **Save** (the right-hand button) when the status says Ready.”

The control’s name and a text status stand alone. Location can still help.

</div>

</div>

## Not a pass

“The round one” plus a color still fails this rule if there is no name or other text. An icon whose only meaning is its shape, with no text alternative, is the same miss. A legend that restates the sensory cue (“right means go”) does not replace a name.
