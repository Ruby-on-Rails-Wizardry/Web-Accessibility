## Why this repo still points at it

- **Taxonomy** — pitch the work: recalling that 508 is WCAG 2.0 AA is not the same as applying 1.3.1 to a form or evaluating a page against a profile.
- **Learning for Mastery** — name the profile, diagnose, prescribe the next surface. Do not treat the first automated scan as a permanent sort.

Neither paper is an accessibility standard. They do not change WCAG, 508, or ADA.
